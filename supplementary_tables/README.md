# Supplementary tables

Tables that are too large for the manuscript. Numbering follows the Supplementary Material of the manuscript.

| File | Content |
| --- | --- |
| `S3_Mash_ANI_matrix_k21_120genomes.tsv` | Mash-based ANI (%) matrix, k = 21, sketch size 10,000, among 120 genomes (Section 4.2.4) |
| `S4_pyANI_ANIm_matrix_120genomes.tsv` | pyANI ANIm (%) matrix among the same 120 genomes (Section 4.2.4) |
| `S6_DEGs.tsv` | Differentially expressed transcripts of *Agave sisalana* (diseased vs healthy): 46,948 transcripts with padj < 0.05 and \|log2FC\| > 1 (24,734 down, 22,214 up in diseased plants). Columns: transcript ID, baseMean, apeglm-shrunken log2FC, lfcSE, p-value, padj, regulation (Section 5.3.1) |
| `S8_module_enrichment_full.tsv` | Complete list of the 964 significant GO biological process terms (FDR < 0.05) across the 13 network modules with at least 10 nodes; columns: module, module nodes, GO ID, term, k, K, p-value, FDR (Section 5.3.3) |

Rows and columns of the two ANI matrices (S3 and S4) are the genome labels (species and strain) of the 120 genomes (66 *A. niger* clade, 22 *A. welwitschiae* clade,
31 *A. tubingensis* clade and strain CCMB 674).
